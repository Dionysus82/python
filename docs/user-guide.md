# User guide

Sign in with a username from the README and the password `Harbour-Demo-2026`. The red banner is on every page. Read it.

## Pages

1. **Management Dashboard** — HKD exposure and the main exceptions in your portfolios.
2. **Customer and Counterparty Search** — name, industry, rating, watchlist, facility and keyword filters.
3. **Customer 360** — profile, facilities, histories, score explanation, documents and actions.
4. **Group 360** — authorised entities, original currencies, and the HKD total.
5. **Ask Risk Intelligence** — a question. Citations open the source viewer.
6. **Customer Risk Report Generator** — HTML, PDF and JSON drafts.
7. **Portfolio Risk View** — customers ranked by HKD exposure, with industry and jurisdiction charts.
8. **Facilities and Exposure** — one row per facility, with rate and data quality.
9. **Limits and Covenants** — breaches, near-breaches and unresolved figures.
10. **Collateral** — eligible amounts and coverage. Coverage does not cancel other risks.
11. **Early-Warning Indicators** — open and historical indicators.
12. **AML and Financial-Crime Indicators** — visible only with permission, and always marked for human review.
13. **Trusted Documents** — list and source viewer.
14. **Upload and Ingestion** — PDF, DOCX, XLSX, CSV or TXT with the synthetic declaration.
15. **Data Quality and Freshness** — conflicts, stale documents and ingestion exceptions.
16. **RAG Evaluation** — runs the seeded cases and shows the measured rates.
17. **AI Governance** — purpose, models, prohibited uses, kill switch state.
18. **Audit Activity** — recent events, including denials and AML access.
19. **Administration** — users for the platform administrator, and the kill switch for the model administrator.

If a page says the customer is outside your portfolio, do not retry with a reworded question. The scope will not change.
